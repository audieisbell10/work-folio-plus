        How the dive works
        
    
          
      
        • Daily dive: seven questions, the same for everyone, refreshed at 00:00 UTC.
          • 
      
        • Open water prompts ask you to “name a ___”. The first valid answer locks in. Obvious answers score little; rare ones score big. A miss costs 3 seconds, so keep guessing.
          • 
      
        • Sonar quiz questions give four choices. Answer faster for more points. Each wrong pick costs 5 seconds and halves the haul.
          • 
      
        • Every point sinks the probe 10 m. Landmarks along the way are real: dive records, the Titanic, the deepest fish ever filmed.
          • 
      
        • Endless dive: three oxygen tanks. A question that scores nothing costs one tank.
